# Highest-Value Client Audit

Audit your clients to find the patterns behind your *best* ones — where they came
from, what they cost, what they're worth, and how good the relationship is. The
goal: see which acquisition source, service, and avatar produce your most
valuable, longest-lasting, happiest clients, then do more of that.

Fill one row per client. Rate **Results** and **Enjoyment** 1-5 (★).

## What to look for once it's filled

- **Best source:** which acquisition channel produces the highest-LTV clients?
- **Best-fit service/avatar:** which combination has the highest results +
  enjoyment + retention?
- **Hidden costs:** where is fulfillment cost eating the margin?
- **Referral engines:** who refers, and what do they have in common?
- **Double down / drop:** which client *type* to pursue more of, and which to stop
  taking.
