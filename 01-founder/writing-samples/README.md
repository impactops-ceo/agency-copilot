# Writing Samples

Drop 3-5 real examples of your writing here — emails, social posts, sales
messages, docs, anything you wrote yourself. Plain text, markdown, PDF, or Word
all work.

Claude reads these to learn your voice, then summarizes it under **Writing Style**
in `../CONTEXT.md`. From then on, drafts sound like you wrote them.

The more varied the samples (a casual DM, a polished email, a longer post), the
better the match.
