# Speaking Transcripts

Drop transcripts of you talking here — YouTube videos, podcast appearances, webinar
recordings, sales call recordings, voice notes. Export the transcript as text and
drop it in.

Claude reads these to learn how you actually talk — your phrasing, rhythm, and the
stories you tell — then summarizes it under **Speaking Style** in `../CONTEXT.md`.
This is what lets Claude write scripts and talking points that sound natural coming
out of your mouth.
