# thank-you-page

Write the page that turns a conversion into momentum — confirm, set expectations,
and move them to the next step. The most under-used page in the funnel.

## When to use
- "Write a thank-you / confirmation page for [funnel]."
- After building an opt-in or checkout page.

## Process

### Step 1: Load context (RULE 1)
- **The preceding step:** `03-acquisition/CONTEXT.md` + the relevant page in
  `funnels/` → what they just did (opted in, booked, bought) and what *should*
  happen next in the sales process.
- **Voice:** `01-founder/CONTEXT.md`.
- **Avatar & offer:** for the right next-step ask.

### Step 2: SOP — follow or capture (RULE 3)
Check `05-operations/playbook/sops/thank-you-page.md`. If present, follow it. If
not, ask: "What's the ideal next action after this step — book a call, watch a VSL,
join a group, buy an upsell?" Save as the SOP, then proceed.

### Step 3: Apply the framework
1. **Confirm** — reassure the action worked ("You're in").
2. **Set expectations** — what happens next and *when* (email incoming, link below,
   timeline). Reduce uncertainty.
3. **Next step (the point)** — the single highest-value action now: book a call,
   watch the VSL, join the community, or take an ascension/upsell offer. Make it
   obvious and easy.
4. **Optional bonus / reinforcement** — a quick win or reassurance to cement the
   decision.
5. **Tracking note** — flag where conversion pixels / event tracking should fire.

### Step 4: Draft to file
Save to `03-acquisition/funnels/thankyou_<funnel>.md` with copy + the next-step CTA
+ the tracking note.

### Step 5: Review
Confirm the next step matches the real sales process and the expectation-setting is
accurate (right email, right timeline).

## Quality check
- [ ] Confirms the action and sets clear expectations
- [ ] Drives one high-value next step tied to the sales process
- [ ] Owner's voice; tracking note included
- [ ] Saved to `03-acquisition/funnels/`
