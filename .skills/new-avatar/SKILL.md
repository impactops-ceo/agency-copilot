# new-avatar

Spin up a full avatar worksheet for a new ideal client and fill it through a short
interview, using the avatar's own language throughout.

## When to use

- "Add an avatar for [type of client]."
- A new sub-niche or ideal-client segment.

## Process

### Step 1: Create the folder
Copy `02-company/avatars/_template-avatar/` to a new folder named for the avatar
in kebab-case (e.g. `02-company/avatars/agency-owners/`). Replace `{{AVATAR_NAME}}`
in the worksheet.

### Step 2: Walk the worksheet
Interview through `avatar-worksheet.md` conversationally — Niching Down, problems
& fears, desires & goals, frustrations, demographics, psychographics, market
sophistication and awareness. Capture the avatar's **exact words** for problems and
desires; that language is the point.

### Step 3: Connect to the offer
Fill "How your offer addresses the above," linking each major problem/frustration
to what the agency provides.

### Step 4: Register it
Add the avatar to the **Avatars** list in `02-company/CONTEXT.md`, noting which
services target it. Update the `2a Avatars` row in `.onboarding/progress.md` if
relevant.

## Quality check
- [ ] Avatar located precisely (market → sub-market → niche → delivery → avatar)
- [ ] Problems/desires captured in the avatar's own language
- [ ] Awareness and sophistication levels set, with reasoning
- [ ] Listed in `02-company/CONTEXT.md`
