## 1. What it is

- **Impact Ops** is the company (an operations agency, run by founder Jordan
  Armstrong, jordan@impactops.io). Impact Ops builds and runs **truthOS**.
- **truthOS** is the product: one place to run a whole coaching/agency business, with
  a built-in **founder AI** that helps run it. It pulls everything together, watches
  it, tells each person what to do next, and does a lot of the work itself.
- Positioning is **done-with-you / partner-led**, not self-serve software. "You don't
  have to run a software company. We are the dev team."

## 2. The thesis (one line everything ladders to)

**truthOS enforces truth across your business, eliciting the flywheel of trust.**

- Enforce truth: one source of truth per number, real data not cherry-picked proof,
  the same figures shared openly. The founder AI reads the actual situation, not a
  story about it.
- The flywheel of trust: truth compounds into trust, and trust is the growth engine.
  Clients trust the data, so they trust you, so they stay and refer, which becomes
  proof that earns the next clients faster.

**Brand pillars:** Truth. Trust. Transparency.
**Hero promise:** "Built on truth, trust and transparency."
**Core tension line (used as a red "warning" on the site):** "You won't scale what
you don't trust." (Note: this was changed from "can't" to "won't" by owner request.)

the bottleneck and wants out of the day-to-day. The buyer is "great at sales and
marketing, not at operations" and wants to "stop being a prison of other people's
business."

## 4. The platform

- **Four parts:** command centers (pre-set role dashboards), dashboards (custom
  views), the **founder AI** (the branded assistant; on the site it is named
  **"Founder AI"** generically — earlier mockups used "Neo AI" / "Eddie AI" but the
  owner standardized on **Founder AI**), and portals (client-facing login).
- All on **one isolated org data layer**, configured per org type.
- **Six command centers:** Client acquisition, Client success, Hiring, Project
  management, Financial, Operator.
- **"A dashboard shows you the data. A command center lets you use AI to act on it."**
- **The moat / product flywheel** (the owner reframed "the moat" into a flywheel):
  every interaction makes the product better. Four loops:
  1. Every question is tracked (patterns, objections, where clients get stuck).
  2. Every resource is stored and usable (calls, frameworks, SOPs the AI can cite).
  3. Feedback becomes a tracked action item with an owner and deadline.
  4. Wins are captured and turned into testimonials/case studies automatically.
  The dynamic client journey and the founder AI still exist throughout, they're just
  no longer the headline of that section.
- **Core feature line:** "Make the product so good, growth is inevitable."

## 5. Vertical integration (how we work)

The flagship engagement framing: **introduce vertical integration to the client's
business model.** We find the software gap in their market, build the product on
their brand, and add it to their offer for recurring revenue, stickiness, and a
stronger irresistible offer. Clients' data/progress live in the software, so leaving
means losing it.

Everyone starts with an **AI readiness / opportunity audit**:
1. Scan the market for the software gap.
2. Map the recurring revenue opportunity.
3. Design the vertical integration into the offer.
4. Build it on truthOS, the client's brand.

## 6. The offer and pricing

- **Public site shows NO pricing.** Everything routes to the audit or booking a call.
  This is deliberate (owner decision). Don't "fix" the missing pricing.
- Entry path ("walk before we fly"): AI readiness assessment → the setup guide
  (connect the business to truthOS) → the platform goes live (with a call to review
  the audit) → build with us (apply to a partnership).
- **Two partnership tracks (application only). Figures are INTERNAL, kept off the
  public site:**
  - **Impact Incubator** — apply for a **$25,000 scholarship**; selected founders are
    grandfathered into a **12.5% of profit** partnership. We evaluate the
    vertical-integration opportunity and build truthOS out for them.
  - **Visionary Impact Partnership (VIP)** — a **$50,000 investment for 1% of
    truthOS**; we build it over six months, then either train their team to run it or
    run it for them.

Note: earlier in the project there was a different "core build" offer ($25k over six
months, $10k up front + $3k/mo, plus a $7/$27/$97 low-ticket funnel and a 60-day
guarantee). The owner **removed all of that** from the site — no guarantee, no
delivery variants, no enterprise pricing tier, no build price. Treat the Incubator +
VIP tracks above as current.

## 8. Brand and visual identity

- **Theme: dark, premium, flat.** Deep navy canvas (`#060912`) with the **truthOS blue
  gradient** (`#5b8cff → #2fe1e6`, blue-to-cyan). This is the locked brand (the owner
  tried a light "Viktor.com-style" cream/purple direction, then reverted to the dark
  truthOS palette while keeping the Viktor *structure*: contained sections, big
  rounded cards, white accent cards/sections to break up the dark, a giant footer
  wordmark).
- **Status colors:** good/green `#46d9a0`, warning/red `#ff6b7a`.
- **Typography:** Inter (the rounded Fredoka/Nunito pairing was tried and rejected).
- **Logo:** a "t-in-circle" monogram (`website/assets/logo.svg`) recolored to the
  brand gradient, used with the `truthOS` wordmark. No tagline under it.
- **Name casing (non-negotiable):** always `truthOS` — lowercase t, capital OS, even
  at the start of a sentence. Never TruthOS / Truthos / TruthOs.
- Full tokens and component rules: `website/brand-guide.md`.

## 9. Writing rules (judge every word against these)

- Always `truthOS` casing.
- **No em dashes** and no spaced-hyphen dashes — use a period, a comma, or "and".
- Sentence-case headings (not Title Case).
- Voice: direct, plain, certain, founder to colleague. Short sentences. Concrete over
  abstract. Lead with the mechanism. No hype-stacking, no jargon.
