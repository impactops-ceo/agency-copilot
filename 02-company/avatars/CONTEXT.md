# Avatars

Your ideal clients — the specific people you serve. Each avatar gets its own
worksheet so Claude can speak their language, target them precisely, and write
copy that converts.

Built on **self-congruity theory**: people buy what reflects their own self-image,
values, and beliefs. The better you understand an avatar, the better you convert
them. Using *their* language — their exact problems, fears, and desires — is what
makes a message land.

## What's here

| File/Folder | What |
|-------------|------|
| `_template-avatar/` | Blank worksheet. Copy it for each new avatar. |
| `<avatar-name>/` | One folder per avatar, each with a filled `avatar-worksheet.md` |

## How to add an avatar

- **In conversation:** run the `new-avatar` skill, or just say "add an avatar."
- **By hand:** copy `_template-avatar/` to a new folder named for the avatar (e.g.
  `agency-owners/`), then fill in `avatar-worksheet.md`.

If different services target different avatars, note that in
`../CONTEXT.md` under **Services** and **Avatars**.
