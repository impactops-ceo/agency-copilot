## Origin Story
_How and why did the company start? The real story you'd tell on a podcast._

Impact Ops ran operations for coaching, agency and expert businesses and saw the same
thing every time: founders great at sales and marketing, stuck in fulfillment,
scattered across a dozen tools, sitting on a goldmine they were giving away. So they
built truthOS, the platform they wished existed: not another dashboard that shows you
the data, but a command center that lets you use AI to act on it. "You don't have to
run a software company. We are the dev team."

## Services Provided
_What do you actually sell? List each service, what it includes, and the price._

Done-with-you / partner-led. Everyone starts with an **AI readiness / opportunity
audit**. The flagship engagement is **vertical integration**: we find the software gap
in a client's market, build the product on their brand, and add it to their offer for
recurring revenue and retention. Two application-only partnership tracks (internal
figures, not shown publicly):
- **Impact Incubator** — $25,000 scholarship, grandfathered into a 12.5% of profit
  partnership.
- **Visionary Impact Partnership (VIP)** — $50,000 for 1% of truthOS; six-month build,
  then train-their-team or run-it-for-them.

The public site shows **no pricing** by design; everything routes to the audit or a
call. See `truthos-knowledge.md` §6.
