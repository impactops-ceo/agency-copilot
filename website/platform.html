The agentic layer

Agents that own the work.

Describe what you want in plain language. truthOS builds the skill, connects your tools, and runs it for you, every time. Pick an example to see how a skill comes together.

Before every Calendly sales call, research the account in ZoomInfo, update the HubSpot opportunity, and prepare a tailored briefing.
1Watch Calendly for new bookings.
2Pull firmographics and buying signals from ZoomInfo.
3Draft a one-page briefing in your format.
4Update the HubSpot opportunity with the research.
5Send the briefing to the rep before the call.
When a Stripe invoice is 7 days overdue, draft a friendly follow-up in Gmail in our voice and flag it for me.
1Watch Stripe for overdue invoices.
2Draft a follow-up in your voice.
3Attach the invoice and a payment link.
4Flag it for your approval.
5Send and log the outcome to the record.
Every Monday, build the weekly numbers from truthOS, write the recap, and post it to Slack.
1Pull the week's numbers from the data layer.
2Compare to plan and flag what moved.
3Write the recap in your voice.
4Post it to #leadership.
5Log the decisions back to the data layer.
Route every new HubSpot lead to the closer with the best fit and the highest close rate this week, and notify them in Slack.
1Watch HubSpot for new leads.
2Score fit against your ICP.
3Check each closer's live close rate.
4Assign and notify them in Slack.
5Track the lead to first call.
