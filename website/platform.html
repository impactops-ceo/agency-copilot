The foundation

One org data layer.

Everything is tied to one business and isolated to it: calls, numbers, clients, your team's work, messages. One source of truth per number, the same figures shared openly. The founder AI reads the actual situation, not a story about it.

The platform is then configured per org type. A coaching business and an agency get different recipes of the same parts.
