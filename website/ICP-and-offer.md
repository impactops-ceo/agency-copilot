# truthOS — ICP and offer reference (for website review)

A self-contained brief for reviewing the truthOS marketing site. It covers who the
site is for (the ideal client profile and three avatars), how truthOS is positioned,
the offer and partnership structure, the proof, and the brand and writing rules to
judge copy against. A reviewer should be able to evaluate every page against this
without any other document.

The live site to review is in this repo under `website/` (pages: home, platform,
who-its-for, proof, pricing/"Work with us", about/"Our truth", book-a-call).

---

## 1. The one line everything ladders up to

**truthOS enforces truth across your business, eliciting the flywheel of trust.**

- **Enforces truth:** one source of truth per number, real data instead of
  cherry-picked proof, the same figures shared openly. The founder AI reads the
  actual situation, not a story about it.
- **The flywheel of trust:** truth compounds into trust, and trust is the growth
  engine. Clients trust the data, so they trust you, so they stay and refer, which
  becomes real proof that earns the next clients faster.

**Brand pillars:** Truth. Trust. Transparency.
**Hero promise:** "Built on truth, trust and transparency."
**Core tension line:** "You won't scale what you don't trust."

## 2. Ideal client profile (ICP)

truthOS serves **coaching, agency, and expert businesses**. One shared truth across
every buyer: **the founder is the bottleneck and wants out of the day-to-day.**

**The buyer in one line:** great at sales and marketing, not at operations, and
wanting to "stop being a prison of other people's business."

**Qualifiers**
- Has an audience, an offer that works, and revenue (roughly $30k/mo and up).
- Genius and frameworks are trapped in calls and videos, not productized.
- Has tried "AI brain" tools and found them toys.
- Does not want to run a software company.

**Disqualifiers**
- Pre-revenue or no audience or distribution.
- Looking for cheap software they self-implement (this is done-with-you, partner-led).
- Not willing to start with an audit.

## 3. The three avatars

### Avatar A — The expert with a personal brand
- **Who:** coaches, course creators, founders with an audience. ~$30k to $300k/mo.
  (e.g. Sean / Royalty Hero.)
- **Core pain:** can't deliver more without working more; genius scattered across
  videos and calls; every "AI brain" was a toy.
- **What they want:** productize the program so the work happens without them in every
  call; recurring revenue; keep their voice.
- **Their fear:** "Will the AI sound like me or generic?" "I don't want to run a
  software company."
- **truthOS answer:** productizes the program, recurring revenue, a founder AI that
  sounds like them. "You don't have to run a software company. We are the dev team."

### Avatar B — The agency owner or elite coach
- **Who:** agencies and top coaches. ~$100k to $500k/mo. (e.g. Gil, Andy, Cameron.)
- **Core pain:** on the treadmill, low retainers, high churn, signing clients just to
  stay flat; scattered stack; stuck in fulfillment.
- **What they want:** off the treadmill; productize delivery so margin stops scaling
  with headcount; one dashboard; place trained operators.
- **Their fear:** "AI wipes out agencies." "What happens after the build, am I stuck
  paying forever?"
- **truthOS answer:** productized delivery, one dashboard for the whole roster,
  trained operators, an owned platform that keeps getting more valuable.

### Avatar C — The enterprise authority
- **Who:** the biggest names with real audience and distribution. Seven figures a
  month and up. (e.g. Eddie Malouf.)
- **Core pain:** move slower than their own ideas; community sits on unproductized
  knowledge; won't run a software company.
- **What they want:** a tech partner with dev velocity to match their ideas; a
  white-labeled OS in their brand; enterprise value and a path to exit.
- **Their fear:** "Working with amateurs is brand risk." "Can I trust this team with
  my name?"
- **truthOS answer:** a tech partner that moves at their speed, a white-labeled OS, an
  equity-grade partnership (see VIP below).

## 4. What truthOS is (plain terms)

One place to run your whole business, with a smart assistant that helps run it. It
pulls everything together, watches it for you, tells each person what to do next, and
does a lot of the work itself.

Four things it does:
1. Puts everything in one place (calls, numbers, clients, team's work, messages).
2. Tells you what needs you today.
3. Does the work with you (write the email, build the report, make the payment link).
4. Gets smarter the longer you use it. And it's yours.

**Key product line:** "A dashboard shows you the data. A command center lets you use
AI to act on it."

**The platform parts:** command centers (pre-set role dashboards), dashboards (custom
views), the founder AI (branded assistant, e.g. Eddie AI / Neo AI), and portals
(client-facing login). All on one isolated org data layer, configured per org type.

**The six command centers:** Client acquisition, Client success, Hiring, Project
management, Financial, Operator.

**The moat (why they can't leave):**
- The dynamic client journey: reads the first call, lays out the client's moves in
  order, advances as they finish, keeps the AI pointed at the real constraint.
- The founder AI: trained on the founder's calls, videos and frameworks, answers in
  their voice, benchmarks the client's data against everyone else's.

## 5. The offer and how we work

**Positioning:** done-with-you, partner-led. We find the software opportunity in the
client's market, then build it with them. "You don't have to run a software company.
We are the dev team."

**Everyone starts with an audit** (the AI readiness / opportunity audit): map their
operations, their genius, and the software opportunity in their niche. Example:
Royalty Hero, where we evaluated the market, picked the most lucrative product to own
(an all-in-one book writing and promotion tool), and built CoAuthor AI, which added a
recurring revenue layer with no paid ads.

**What they get (productized coaching / product-led coaching):** a custom course or
coaching product on their domain, the founder AI trained on their content and voice,
client goal and progress tracking, an indexed content library, the AI note-taker, the
dynamic client journey, a member directory, and client portals.

**The path on the site ("walk before we fly"):**
1. AI readiness assessment
2. The setup guide (connect the business to truthOS)
3. The platform goes live (with a call to review the readiness audit)
4. Build with us (apply to a partnership)

### Two partnership tracks (application only)

> **Pricing is intentionally NOT shown on the public site.** Everything pushes to the
> audit and booking a call. The figures below are internal, for the reviewer's
> context only. Do not flag the site for "missing pricing" — that is by design.

- **Impact Incubator** *(internal terms: apply for a $25,000 scholarship; selected
  founders are grandfathered into a 12.5% of profit deal)*: we evaluate the
  vertical-integration software opportunity in their market and build truthOS out for
  them, in a long-term profit-share partnership.
- **Visionary Impact Partnership (VIP)** *(internal terms: $50,000 investment for 1%
  of truthOS)*: an equity partnership for the biggest names. We build their truthOS
  over six months, then either train their team to run it or run it for them.

**Removed and should NOT appear:** the old $25k build price, the $10k + $3k/mo terms,
delivery variants, the 60-day guarantee, and a public "enterprise pricing" tier.

## 7. Brand, voice and writing rules (judge every word against these)

**Name and casing (non-negotiable):** always write `truthOS` (lowercase t, capital
OS). Never TruthOS, Truthos, or TruthOs, even at the start of a sentence.

**Voice:** direct, plain, certain, founder to colleague. Short sentences. Concrete
over abstract. Lead with the mechanism. No hype-stacking, no jargon.

**Hard rules:**
- No em dashes (use a period, a comma, or "and").
- No AI-vocabulary or filler.
- Sentence-case headings (not Title Case).

**Visual system:** dark, premium, flat, lots of whitespace. Deep navy background with
a blue-to-cyan "truthOS blue" gradient accent. Full tokens are in
`website/brand-guide.md`.

## 9. What to focus a review on

- Does each page speak to one or more of the three avatars in their own words?
- Is the audit-first, partner-led offer clear, with no stray pricing?
- Does the copy hold the voice and the hard rules (casing, no em dashes, sentence
  case)?
- Is the thesis (truth to trust) felt, not just stated?
- Is the single CTA (book a call) obvious on every page?
